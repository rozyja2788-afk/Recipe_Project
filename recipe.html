MY FAVORITE WHITE RICE AND CHICKEN STEW RECIPE

INGREDIENTS

  • Rice (3 cups)
  • Tomatoes(fresh and Tin) 500g
  • Fresh pepper 250g
  • Salt (half teaspoon)
  • Onion (2pieces)
  • Spices (curry,Thyme,bellpepper)
  • Chicken (2kilos)
  • Groundnut oil (500g)
  • Crayfish (150g)

INSTRUCTIONS

FOR RICE

  1. Rinse the rice thoroughly in clean water remove excess starch
  2. Pour water into a pot and bring it to a boil.
  3. Add the rinsed rice, a pinch of salt, and allow it to cook on medium heat.
  4. Cook until the grains are soft and all the water is absorbed.
  5. Set aside to keep warm.

FOR CHICKEN STEW

  1. Blend tomatoes, onions, and peppers until smooth.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pot and pour in the blended mixture.
  3. Add groundnut oil,onions and crayfish and allow to heat
  4. Bring a pot, put your chicken inside and add spices to marinate the chicken taste allow to heat on medium heat
  5. pour in the blended mixture(pepper,tomatoes and onions) in the preheated groundnut oil mixture.
  6. Allow it to fry for about 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Add seasoning cubes, salt, and spices to taste.
  8. Add the cooked marinated chicken for another 5 minutes.
  9. After 10 minutes,bring down and serve


SERVING

  • Scoop rice onto a plate.
  • Pour the stew generously over the rice.
  • Serve hot with plantains, salad, or vegetables on the side.

  • Ensure to stir the mixture continuously to avoid burning the tomatoes
